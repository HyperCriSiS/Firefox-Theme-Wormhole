How your images fit together

  • Your picture: its top strip is painted behind tabs and toolbars, anchored top right, and the new tab page shows the rest directly below, so they always line up. Keep the main motif on the right.
  • New tab background: fills the page, cropped from the center. Its bottom fades into the color chosen above. Waterfox only: set the same color for the status bar in userChrome.css (see below).
  • Menu and accent color: pick them to match your picture, so menus and the address bar fit it. A light menu color switches the browser to dark text for light pictures. The AI prompt below suggests all three colors.

Your images are stored only in this browser and never uploaded.

Create matching images with AI

Copy this prompt into an AI image tool and replace the last line with your idea. It asks for one wide picture, dark or light, with the safe areas and fades this theme needs.

Remove the last edges (optional)

Add-ons cannot change browser files, so this step is manual. The optional userChrome.css removes the line between toolbars and page, matches menus to the theme and, in Waterfox, removes the edge above the status bar. Waterfox only: the status bar color is the --wormhole-statusbar-color line at the top of userChrome.css; set it to the same color as the fade above.
