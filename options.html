How your images fit together

  • Header image: painted behind tabs and toolbars, anchored top right at its original size. The new tab page continues the same image below the toolbars, so they always line up. Use about 3840 × 320 px and keep the main motif on the right.
  • Transition: the continuation fades into the new tab background. It looks seamless when the bottom of the header and the top of the background share colors.
  • New tab background: fills the page, cropped from the center. Its bottom fades into very dark blue (#02050c), the color of the Waterfox status bar.
