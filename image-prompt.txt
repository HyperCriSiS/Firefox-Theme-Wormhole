Create one wide landscape image for a browser theme. The theme cuts it into two parts itself: the top strip goes behind the browser tabs and toolbars, the rest becomes the new tab page. Both parts come from the same picture, so they always line up.

THE IMAGE
- Landscape, as wide as you can make it: 16:9 or 2:1. If you can, make it at least 1536 px wide.
- No text, no logos, no watermarks, no UI elements, no frames, no borders.
- One continuous picture: no hard horizontal line anywhere.

WHERE THINGS GO
- Top 15 %: tabs, buttons and the address bar sit on top of it. Keep it calm, without bright spots, faces or busy detail behind the left half, where the tab titles sit. Sky, clouds, glow or leaves may reach into it, and on the right the motif may go up to the top edge.
- Left 15 %: a browser sidebar may cover it. Keep it calm, only sky, water, shadow or foliage, with no objects that would be cut off.
- Main motif in the right third, vertically around the middle. Narrow windows cut the picture on the left, so nothing important there.
- Center, about 8 % above the bottom edge: a search field sits there. Keep that area calm.
- Bottom 5 %: fade into one solid color (the BOTTOM COLOR), so the page ends without an edge.

DARK OR LIGHT
Pick one and keep the whole picture consistent:
- Dark (default): night, dusk or a darkish scene. Light text sits on top of it.
- Light: daylight, pastel or misty. Dark text sits on top of it; avoid pure white areas.

AFTER THE IMAGE
Give me three hex colors taken from the finished image:
1. BOTTOM COLOR: the color the bottom edge fades into. I enter it in "Color the new tab page fades into" on the theme's settings page. Waterfox only: also in the line --wormhole-statusbar-color at the top of userChrome.css.
2. MENU COLOR: a calm tone from the top of the image for menus, panels and the address bar. Dark picture: a dark tone (brightness 10 to 25 %), light text must be easy to read on it. Light picture: a light tone (brightness 85 to 95 %), dark text must be easy to read on it.
3. ACCENT COLOR: the most striking color of the motif, for the selected tab and focused fields. It must stand out clearly on the MENU COLOR.
